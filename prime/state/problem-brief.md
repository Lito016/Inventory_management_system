# Problem Brief

## Problem Statement
The client operates two business lines — B2B fabric trading and B2C printing — using manual processes for financial tracking, order management, and inventory. This results in:

1. **Manual receipt tracking** — physical receipts are the primary record, difficult to search or verify
2. **Manual calculations** — customer/supplier balances computed by hand, prone to errors
3. **No centralized financial view** — receivables, payables, and historical debts scattered across papers/spreadsheets
4. **Duplicate data entry** — same customer/supplier info entered separately for each business line
5. **No order-to-delivery tracking** — B2B ordered vs. received quantities not systematically tracked
6. **Historical debt opacity** — old debts not recorded or verified, creating financial uncertainty

## Impact
- Financial errors directly affect cash flow and business relationships
- Time wasted on manual computation reduces productive business hours
- Inability to quickly retrieve transaction records hampers decision-making
- No visibility into outstanding receivables/payables leads to collection delays

## Root Cause
The business lacks a unified digital system. Current tools (paper, spreadsheets) cannot enforce data consistency, automate calculations, or provide cross-business-line visibility.

## Proposed Solution
A unified web-based system (UBMS) that:
- Centralizes customer, supplier, and financial data
- Automates balance calculations and payment tracking
- Provides structured workflows for both B2B and B2C operations
- Records and tracks historical debts with verification status
- Makes all transactions searchable and exportable

- **Pain severity:** High (financial operations are core business)
- **Market clarity:** Clear (specific business with known workflows)
- **Technical feasibility:** High (standard web app with Supabase backend)
- **ROI potential:** High (immediate reduction in manual work and errors)
